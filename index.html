Worlds

Every few stages, a new place to fight in

These are three of the many biomes. Each one changes the look, the foes and the platforms. Clear every foe on the stage to open the way forward.

Jungle

STG 1/100

Wooden crates, grassy ledges and fast orange raiders. A gentle start.

Glacier

STG 3/100

Frozen pines and ice shooters. Ten foes to clear before the gate opens.

Swamp

STG 5/100

Murky ground and the Tyrant Rex, with its own health bar across the top of the screen.

+

Tons more biomes

Keep climbing toward stage 100 to find them all.

What you get

Built for quick runs and long grinds

Level up as you go

Defeat foes, gain levels and watch your health bar grow from stage to stage.

Four weapon slots

Swap between swords on the fly with the hotbar at the bottom of the screen.

Score and coins

Chase your best score, collect coins and track your best stage on the home screen.

Tons of bosses

Bosses like the Tyrant Rex bring a big health bar and a lot more bite.
